1. Closed-form beam theory
  uniform cantilever, mesh refinement (each row converged to 1%)
     nodes  segments  substeps   tip (mm)  vs analytic
         9         8        16      78.10        24.8%
        17        16        64      70.30        12.3%
        33        32      2048      26.41       -57.8%  (not converged)
    analytic qL^4/(8EI) = 62.58 mm
    The residual offset is the discrete model's own bias: the soil
    clamp is an elastic hinge rather than a rigid encastre, and mass
    is lumped at nodes. Both make the chain slightly more compliant
    than the continuum beam the closed form describes.

2. Solver convergence
  solver convergence, uniform 17-node cantilever
     substeps  iters   work   tip (mm)
            8      2     16     307.53
            8      8     64     114.36
            8     32    256      71.03
            8    128   1024      69.17
           16      4     64      85.49
           32      4    128      70.48
           64      4    256      70.31
          128      4    512      70.17
    Both knobs converge eventually, but not at the same price:
    64 substeps x 4 iterations (work 256) matches what 8 substeps
    x 128 iterations (work 1024) achieves -- a 4x saving for the
    same answer. Hence substeps=32, iterations=4 as the default.
